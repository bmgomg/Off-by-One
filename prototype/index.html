Off by One

Just a slide of hand.
One row or column has been shifted out of place. Drag a row sideways or a column up or down to put it back. Tiles wrap around the edge, and you get one shift. Keyboard: arrows pick a card, Shift+arrows slide its lane, Enter commits, Esc cancels.
